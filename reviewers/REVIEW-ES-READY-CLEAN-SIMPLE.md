### 0314 — La cabalgata de Reyes

- **lit.** "the ride/parade of the Kings"
- **meaning:** The evening parade of January 5th in which the Three Kings appear in person — on floats, camels, or horseback — throwing sweets to the crowd along the route. It's the emotional peak of the whole season for children, who wake up to their gifts the next morning.
