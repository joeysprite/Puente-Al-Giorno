### 0313 — Pedir posada

- **lit.** "to ask for lodging"
- **meaning:** To take part in the Mexican Advent ritual las posadas (16–24 December): each night a procession reenacts Mary and Joseph's search for shelter, going door to door and singing a call-and-response litany asking to be let in, until one house finally opens and the night's celebration begins.
- **example:** Cada noche del 16 al 24 de diciembre, los vecinos salen a pedir posada por la calle.

### 0327 — El aguinaldo

- **lit.** "the Christmas bonus"
- **meaning:** The year-end bonus Mexican employers must pay by 20 December, by law at least fifteen days' wages. In a posada, 'aguinaldo' is also the small bag of candy and fruit handed to guests.
