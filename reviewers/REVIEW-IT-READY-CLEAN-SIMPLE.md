### 0313 — La novena di Natale

- **lit.** "the Christmas novena"
- **meaning:** Nine days of prayer and song leading up to Christmas (16–24 December) — the same nine days, marking the nine months Jesus spent in Mary's womb. In its most visible form, especially in the South (Naples, Sicily, Abruzzo), it is carried by travelling zampognari — bagpipers — who play from house to house or in front of nativity scenes; further north it survives mainly as sung prayer inside church. There is no door-to-door 'ask and be turned away' dialogue: the Italian novena is a devotion performed, not a story reenacted.
- **example:** Durante la novena di Natale, gli zampognari suonano davanti al presepe.
