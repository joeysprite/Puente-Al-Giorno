El puente · Il ponte

Spanish en persona does two jobs; Italian splits them — di persona for showing up yourself, in persona for 'the very man himself'. The Spanish speaker's import lands on the emphatic one by accident.

La trampa · La trappola

ES → IT

EN pulls 'in persona' — which exists in Italian but leans emphatic ('the director himself'); the plain 'face to face' sense is di persona.

IT → ES

DI pulls 'de persona' — not Spanish. En persona covers both jobs.
