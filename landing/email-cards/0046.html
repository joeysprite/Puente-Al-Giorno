El puente · Il ponte

The same soothing instruction built by two different machines: Italian softens by SAYING IT TWICE (piano piano — reduplication), Spanish by SHRINKING THE WORD (despacio becomes despacito, the diminutive). Each language reaches for its favorite intensity dial. Both also drift into 'little by little' territory — cross-reference 0234 (a poco a poco / poco a poco), and Mexico even reduplicates the diminutive: poquito a poquito.

La trampa · La trappola

ES → IT

Spanish speakers under-use Italian reduplication — it feels childlike to them, but piano piano, pian piano, zitto zitto, sotto sotto are fully adult Italian.

IT → ES

Italians importing reduplication produce 'despacio despacio' — understood, marked; the Spanish softener is the diminutive: despacito.
