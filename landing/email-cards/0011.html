Español

Echar leña al fuego

“to throw firewood on the fire”

To make a tense situation worse.

No le eches más leña al fuego, que ya están bastante enfadados.

Don't add more fuel to the fire, they're already angry enough.

Italiano

Gettare benzina sul fuoco

“to throw gasoline on the fire”

To inflame an already tense situation.

Dire questo ora significa gettare benzina sul fuoco.

Saying that now means pouring fuel on the fire.

El puente · Il ponte

Same image, escalating fuel: Spanish adds wood, Italian adds gasoline. gettare recurs (see 0004) — a verb worth banking.

La trampa · La trappola

ES → IT

'Gettare legna sul fuoco' reads as literally adding firewood. The Italian idiom pours fuel on it: benzina.

IT → ES

'Echar gasolina al fuego' is understood but isn't the fixed form. Spanish adds wood: echar leña al fuego.
