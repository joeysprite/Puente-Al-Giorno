Español

Por lo general

“for the general”

Usually; as a rule.

Por lo general me levanto a las siete.

I usually get up at seven.

Italiano

di solito

“of usual”

Usually; as a rule.

Di solito mi alzo alle sette.

I usually get up at seven.

El puente · Il ponte

No bridge survives here — Italian's di solito and Spanish's por lo general share neither preposition nor noun, and the calques fail loudly in both directions. Some crossings you simply swim.

La trampa · La trappola

ES → IT

'De sólito' is not Spanish — the frames don't align at all: por lo general, generalmente, or normalmente.

IT → ES

'Per il generale' is nobody's Italian; the everyday adverbial is di solito (or generalmente, shared).
