Español

Pagar con la misma moneda

“to pay with the same coin”

To pay someone back in kind; to retaliate the same way.

Me ignoró, así que le pagué con la misma moneda.

He ignored me, so I paid him back in kind.

Italiano

Rendere pan per focaccia

“to give back bread for focaccia”

To pay someone back in kind — usually with interest.

Mi ha fatto uno sgarbo e io gli ho reso pan per focaccia.

He slighted me and I gave as good as I got.

El puente · Il ponte

Both settle scores in kind, but the ledgers differ: Spanish pays you back con la misma moneda — exact change — while Italian hands back bread where you gave focaccia: repayment with INTEREST, the coarser loaf for the finer. Italian even has the flat calque 'pagare con la stessa moneta'; the idiomatic form is the older, harsher one.

La trampa · La trappola

ES → IT

Calquing gives 'pagare con la stessa moneta' — real Italian, but flat; the idiomatic form is rendere pan per focaccia, and it threatens MORE than you got, not the same. Note the clipped 'pan' inside the fixed phrase.

IT → ES

Calquing 'devolver pan por focaccia' is not Spanish. Pay them back con la misma moneda.
