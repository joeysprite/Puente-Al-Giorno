Español

En cuanto a

“in how-much to”

As far as … is concerned; regarding.

En cuanto a los costos, decidimos mañana.

As for costs, we'll decide tomorrow.

Italiano

per quanto riguarda

“for how-much it-regards”

As far as … is concerned; regarding.

Per quanto riguarda i costi, decidiamo domani.

As for costs, we decide tomorrow.

El puente · Il ponte

Both build 'as regards' scaffolding, but Italian keeps a live verb in it (riguarda) while Spanish compressed the whole thing down to en cuanto a. The Spanish is shorter than feels safe — trust it.

La trampa · La trappola

ES → IT

Calquing gives 'por cuanto riguarda/respecta' — the everyday Spanish frame is shorter: en cuanto a. (Por lo que respecta a exists, formal.)

IT → ES

Calquing gives 'in quanto a' — which exists but shades different; the workhorse is per quanto riguarda.
