Español

No pegar ojo

“to not stick an eye”

To not sleep a wink.

Con el ruido de la fiesta no pegué ojo en toda la noche.

With the noise from the party I didn't sleep a wink.

Italiano

Non chiudere occhio

“to not close an eye”

To not sleep at all.

Ero così agitato che non ho chiuso occhio.

I was so anxious I didn't sleep a wink.

El puente · Il ponte

Both are negative-polarity idioms: they exist only in the negative. You cannot 'pegar ojo' affirmatively.

La trampa · La trappola

ES → IT

'Non attaccare occhio' — pegar is glue/stick, but Italian closes the eye: non chiudere occhio.

IT → ES

'No cerrar ojo' is understood but isn't the fixed form. Spanish sticks: no pegar ojo.
