Español

Estar como una cabra

“to be like a goat”

To be a bit crazy; said jokingly or affectionately.

Mi vecino está como una cabra, pone música a las tres de la mañana.

My neighbor's nuts, he blasts music at 3am.

Italiano

Essere fuori come un balcone

“to be out like a balcony”

To be out of one's mind.

Ma sei fuori come un balcone? Non puoi guidare così!

Are you out of your mind? You can't drive like that!

El puente · Il ponte

Spanish reaches for an animal, Italian for architecture. The Italian is a pun: a balcony juts fuori ('out'), and essere fuori alone already means to be out of one's mind.

La trampa · La trappola

ES → IT

'Essere come una capra' says something else in Italian — a capra is ignorant, hopeless at something, not crazy. Italian puts you outside instead: fuori come un balcone.

IT → ES

'Estar fuera como un balcón' means nothing in Spanish. The animal carries it: como una cabra.
