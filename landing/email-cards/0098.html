B2

Español

Subirse a la parra

“to climb up the grapevine”

To fly into a rage; to hit the ceiling.

Cuando le dije que no, se subió a la parra.

When I said no, he hit the roof.

Italiano

Uscire dai gangheri

“to come off the hinges”

To fly into a rage; to lose all self-control.

Quando gli ho detto di no, è uscito dai gangheri.

When I said no, he went off the deep end.

El puente · Il ponte

Rage as displacement, in two directions. Spanish sends you UP — climbing the grapevine, out of reach, ranting from somewhere above the conversation. Italian sends you OFF — a door wrenched from its hinges, still recognisably itself but no longer attached to anything. Height versus detachment. And Italian keeps a spare: this is the third temper idiom in the bank, after the swelling nostrils and the fly on the nose.
