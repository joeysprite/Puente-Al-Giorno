Español

Hace poco

“it-makes little”

Recently; a short while ago.

La vi hace poco.

I saw her recently.

Italiano

di recente

“of recent”

Recently; a short while ago.

L'ho vista di recente.

I saw her recently.

El puente · Il ponte

Italian makes recency a prepositional phrase (di recente); Spanish routes it through its time-machine verb hacer (hace poco) — the same hace that tells you how long ago anything happened. Cross-reference entry 0030.

La trampa · La trappola

ES → IT

'Di reciente' is not Spanish — recency runs through hacer: hace poco, the same hace as hace dos años (entry 0030).

IT → ES

'Fa poco' alone is not the Italian marker; di recente or poco fa.
