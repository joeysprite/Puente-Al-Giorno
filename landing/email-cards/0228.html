El puente · Il ponte

Hurry sits IN Italian and comes OF Spanish (de prisa) — with the extra wrinkle that Spanish fused its version into a single word, deprisa, without changing anything. The flip only really bites going it to es.

La trampa · La trappola

ES → IT

DE pulls 'di fretta' — which Italian actually accepts (di fretta exists!) but the neutral form is in fretta. Low-stakes flip.

IT → ES

IN pulls 'en prisa' — not Spanish. De prisa (also spelled deprisa), or MX colloquial 'a las carreras'.
