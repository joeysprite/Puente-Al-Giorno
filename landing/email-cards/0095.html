Español

Empezar la casa por el tejado

“to start the house at the roof”

To do things in the wrong order; to skip the groundwork.

Compró el coche antes de sacarse el carnet: empezó la casa por el tejado.

He bought the car before getting his licence — cart before the horse.

Italiano

Mettere il carro davanti ai buoi

“to put the cart in front of the oxen”

To do things in the wrong order.

Ha comprato la macchina prima della patente: ha messo il carro davanti ai buoi.

He bought the car before the licence — cart before the horse.

El puente · Il ponte

Same error, two industries. Spanish builds: a house begun at the roof, with nothing underneath to hold it — a failure of foundations, and the whole thing will fall. Italian hauls: the cart placed in front of the oxen, everything present and correctly assembled but facing the wrong way, so nothing moves. One image says you skipped a step, the other says you reversed one. English keeps the cart and swaps the animal.

La trampa · La trappola

ES → IT

Roofs are not the Italian image — the mistake is agricultural: il carro davanti ai BUOI, oxen, not horses.

IT → ES

Spanish bueyes exists, but the idiom does not use it — 'poner el carro delante de los bueyes' is understood and not idiomatic. The vivid fixed phrase builds the house from the roof down: empezar la casa por el tejado.
