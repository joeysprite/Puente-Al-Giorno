Español

Buscarle tres pies al gato

“to look for three feet on the cat”

To overcomplicate things; to look for problems that aren't there.

No le busques tres pies al gato, la respuesta es simple.

Don't overcomplicate it, the answer is simple.

Italiano

Cercare il pelo nell'uovo

“to look for the hair in the egg”

To nitpick; to find fault over trivia.

Smettila di cercare il pelo nell'uovo.

Stop nitpicking.

El puente · Il ponte

NOT true equivalents. Both hunt for something that shouldn't exist, but Spanish leans toward overcomplicating, Italian toward pedantic fault-finding. Teach the gap, don't paper over it.

La trampa · La trappola

ES → IT

The idioms are not interchangeable: buscarle tres pies al gato is overcomplicating; cercare il pelo nell'uovo is nitpicking.

IT → ES

An Italian using buscarle tres pies al gato for nitpicking will be heard as describing someone who overcomplicates, not someone who fusses over detail.
