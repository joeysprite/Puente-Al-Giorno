Español

Ser pan comido

“to be eaten bread”

To be very easy.

El examen fue pan comido.

The exam was a piece of cake.

Italiano

Essere un gioco da ragazzi

“to be a kids' game”

To be effortlessly easy.

Per lei, cucinare è un gioco da ragazzi.

For her, cooking is child's play.

El puente · Il ponte

Spanish: bread already eaten — no effort left. Italian: a children's game. Three languages, three different easy-things metaphors (English picks cake).

La trampa · La trappola

ES → IT

'Essere pane mangiato' means nothing in Italian. The easy thing is a children's game: un gioco da ragazzi.

IT → ES

'Ser un juego de niños' is idiomatic Spanish, but the preferred form reaches for bread: ser pan comido.
