Español

Estar entre la espada y la pared

“to be between the sword and the wall”

To be stuck between two bad options.

Sin dinero y sin trabajo, estaba entre la espada y la pared.

Broke and jobless, he was caught between a rock and a hard place.

Italiano

Essere tra l'incudine e il martello

“to be between the anvil and the hammer”

To be trapped between two equally bad outcomes.

Mi trovo tra l'incudine e il martello.

I'm caught between a rock and a hard place.

El puente · Il ponte

Spanish traps you between blade and wall; Italian between anvil and hammer — you are the metal being struck. Both use the 'between two things' frame English fills with rock and hard place.

La trampa · La trappola

ES → IT

'Tra la spada e il muro' isn't Italian. The Italian frame is the forge: incudine e martello.

IT → ES

'Entre el yunque y el martillo' is also standard Spanish. The Italian calque lands — both frames work.
