El puente · Il ponte

Identical grammar, different economies. Both drag a resource toward a thing that is theirs — and the resource tells you what each culture pictures as work. Spain drags EMBERS toward a sardine on the fire: one person, one supper, immediate. Italy diverts WATER toward a mill: property, machinery, a livelihood turning. Selfishness at the scale of a meal versus selfishness at the scale of an industry.

La trampa · La trappola
