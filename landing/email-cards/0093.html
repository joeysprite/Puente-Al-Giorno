Español

Tener mal templada la guitarra

“to have one's guitar badly tuned”

To be in a bad mood; to be out of sorts.

Hoy el jefe tiene mal templada la guitarra.

The boss is in a mood today.

Italiano

Avere la luna storta

“to have the moon crooked”

To be in a bad mood; to be easily irritated.

Oggi il capo ha la luna storta.

The boss is in a mood today.

El puente · Il ponte

A bad mood explained by two different systems. Spanish blames the INSTRUMENT: the guitar is out of tune, so everything the person produces comes out sour — a fault in the works, and fixable. Italian blames the SKY: the moon is crooked today, a condition imposed from outside, nobody's fault and nothing to be done until it passes. One diagnosis suggests tuning; the other suggests waiting.

La trampa · La trappola

ES → IT

Guitars mean nothing to an Italian mood; the moon governs it — avere la luna storta (or essere di luna storta).

IT → ES

'Tener la luna torcida' is not Spanish; the badly tuned guitar carries it.
