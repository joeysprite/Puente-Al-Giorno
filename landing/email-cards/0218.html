Español

Por otro lado

“for other side”

On the other hand.

Por otro lado, los costos suben.

On the other hand, costs are rising.

Italiano

dall'altro lato

“from-the other side”

On the other hand.

Dall'altro lato, i costi salgono.

On the other hand, costs rise.

El puente · Il ponte

Both argue from sides, approached from different directions: Italian comes FROM the other side (da), Spanish goes BY it (por) — and only Italian keeps the article. Discourse markers are where prepositions go feral.

La trampa · La trappola

ES → IT

DA pulls 'de otro lado' — regional at best; the pan-Hispanic marker is POR otro lado, and it drops the article.

IT → ES

POR pulls 'per l'altro lato' — not the marker; Italian arrives FROM the other side: dall'altro lato (or d'altra parte).
