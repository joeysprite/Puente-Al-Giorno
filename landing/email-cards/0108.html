B2

Español

Cantar de plano

“to sing flatly/clearly”

To confess fully, especially to the police.

En cuanto lo interrogaron, cantó de plano.

The moment they questioned him, he spilled everything.

Italiano

Vuotare il sacco

“to empty the sack”

To confess; to get everything off one's chest.

Appena l'hanno interrogato, ha vuotato il sacco.

The moment they questioned him, he emptied the bag.

El puente · Il ponte

Confession as two everyday acts. Spanish SINGS — cantar, the same slang English uses for an informer, the secret coming out as sound whether you meant it or not. Italian EMPTIES THE SACK — vuotare il sacco, tipping out everything you were carrying until nothing's left hidden. One betrays you through the mouth, the other through the hands. Cross-references 0045, where Spanish spits the toad instead.

La trampa · La trappola

Singing is not the Italian image for confession; you empty the sack — vuotare il sacco.

IT → ES

'Vaciar el saco' exists in Spanish but the vivid idiom for confessing is cantar (de plano) — used especially of suspects talking to police, exactly as English 'to sing' does.
